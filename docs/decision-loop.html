The decision loop, from draft to decision

An applicant creates a draft, uploads an identification document and submits. From there a durable workflow takes over. The system runs the checks with external vendors, reliably and asynchronously: it sends the request, and the vendor answers later by calling our webhook. Then it decides: a clean result is approved automatically, and anything flagged goes to a reviewer to approve or decline.
